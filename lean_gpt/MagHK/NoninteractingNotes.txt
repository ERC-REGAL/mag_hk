Noninteracting construction coverage

The module has no declared axioms and no proof placeholders. It proves
concrete statements for real radial functions. Operator realization remains
outside this module.

Core checked constructions

- Gaussian a and its derivative, positive magnetic-field coefficient,
  current cancellation, and scalar factorization.
- Gaussian radial f, its first and second derivative expressions, and the
  literal zero-energy radial differential equation.
- Bounded h, h', h'', R, a_t and U_t with actual derivative theorems.
- The exact positive-margin estimate with numerator
  20*(1-2/(3*sqrt(3)))-11, including its strict positivity.
- Actual positive magnetic-field coefficient away from the axis.
- The regular expression for a_t at the origin and continuity of U_t.
- Integral-defined bounded f_t, positivity, derivative, and literal
  zero-energy radial differential equation.
- Exact f_t/f_0 comparison identity and both pointwise comparison bounds.
- An IVT theorem with explicit continuity and endpoint hypotheses.

Further concrete bounds developed in the module

- f_t <= 1 and f_t <= f_0 on the nonnegative radial half-line.
- U_0 >= 5 for r >= 1, f_0 <= exp(5)*exp(-5*r), and R <= r*exp(r).
- Lower logarithmic bound for the comparison integral.
- Pointwise limits of f_t as t tends to zero from above and to infinity.
- Squared-integrand domination inequalities.

Completed realization chain

BoundedRealization, PlanarSpectralLibrary, AxialRealization, TensorRealization
and FermionRealization connect the concrete orbitals to the actual
antisymmetric ground-state realization. CounterexamplePair and
NoninteractingTheorem include the opposite-field partner and weak gauge
obstruction. OperatorPreliminaries supplies the explicit Sobolev domains
and essential-spectrum formulas. The general operator background remains
explicit, as permitted by the user.

GaussianNormalization.lean proves the actual Gaussian normalization
integral and its unique parameter. NormLimits.lean proves the bounded
radial norm integrability and endpoint limits. ParameterContinuity.lean
and NormContinuity.lean prove continuity and the actual equal-norm
parameter, with no remaining endpoint or continuity assumptions.
Cartesian.lean supplies smooth planar potentials, including the axis.
RadialEndpoints.lean also defines and proves a smooth radial scalar
extension. The spectral and Hilbert-space chain is listed above.

Axis convention

profileV uses Lean's total division at r=0. Its raw value there is not
asserted to be the smooth Cartesian extension of the manuscript formula.
All bounded radial eigen-equations involving profileV require r>0.
The smooth extension is separately defined by regularProfileV in
RadialEndpoints.lean and boundedScalar in Cartesian.lean. Its origin value
is -24-2*t. Both definitions are proved smooth and agree with profileV
away from the origin.

Companion modules

SecondOrbital.lean proves both literal second-orbital radial differential
equations from actual first and second derivatives, including the sqrt(t)
scaling. No derivative equalities are assumed in the final statements.

Axial.lean proves the actual sech orbital differential equation, both
factorization coefficients, positivity, smoothness, bounded potential,
Lebesgue normalization, and integrability of the squared orbital. The
spectral conclusions for the axial operator use separate standard operator
theory inputs.

CylindricalOrbitals.lean proves actual bounded-orbital normalization and
orthogonality with the cylindrical volume element r dr dtheta dz. It
establishes all four pairing integrability conditions, evaluates the
angular phase integral, and applies Fubini with the actual radial and
axial integrals. Its final parameter-existence result assumes only c > 0.

CartesianOrbitals.lean proves that the weighted polar map preserves
Lebesgue measure, including the angular endpoint convention, and defines
the actual orbitals on the Euclidean xy plane represented by C, times R.
It proves their pullback agrees almost everywhere with the cylindrical
formulas and transfers all four pairing integrability conditions and
integrals. Its final exists_bounded_orthonormal theorem has no orbital
normalization or orthogonality assumptions. The Cartesian measure bridge
is therefore closed for these bounded orbitals.

OrbitalDomains.lean proves the concrete finite-energy inputs for the
bounded radial realization. Both f_t and R*f_t are globally smooth, bounded
near the origin, and have positive actual square integrals under r dr.
The actual derivative squares times r and the angular term (R*f_t)^2/r
are integrable. The proof also establishes integrability of the full
magnetic and absolute scalar-potential energy integrands for m=0 and m=-1.
These conclusions use the explicit functions and their proved decay, not
assumed derivative or form-integrability hypotheses. The standard radial
operator realization is handled separately.
