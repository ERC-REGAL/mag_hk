Relative operator realization audit

The operator-theory inputs are propositions passed as theorem arguments.
There are no axiom declarations or supplied instances of these propositions.
The concrete function calculations and their applications are proved in Lean.

OperatorSpectrum.lean
  Spectrum is defined by failure of an actual bounded two-sided inverse on
  the actual operator domain. Multiplicity is the complex dimension of the
  actual eigenspace. SelfAdjoint includes dense domain, complex scalar closure,
  complex linearity, and equality with the Hilbert adjoint graph.

RadialRealization.lean
  StandardRadialFacts supplies general weighted Friedrichs realization,
  weak-derivative and form identities, positive-solution ground transform,
  nonnegative multiplier form comparison, closed first-order product domains,
  partner form identity, and half-line Sturm eigenspace simplicity.
  The endpoint assumptions are visible in CoefficientConditions. They include
  the transformed partner 3/(4r^2)+O(1) endpoint and a tail lower bound.
  RegularSolution includes actual second derivatives, actual finite form
  energy, bounded behavior at zero, actual Lp representation, and positive
  mass. The concrete bounded and Gaussian solutions satisfy these conditions.
  C-infinity compactly supported tests use ContDiff order infinity, not top.

PlanarSpectralLibrary.lean
  StandardPlanarLibrary constructs simultaneous radial and planar realizations
  for arbitrary smooth azimuthal Cartesian coefficients with bounded vector
  potential. Its decaying branch consumes actual a -> 0 and v -> threshold.
  Its confining branch consumes actual v -> infinity.
  It supplies the general polar Fourier action and isometry, direct-sum
  eigenspace facts, and Weyl or compact-resolvent discreteness statements.
  These are the standard operator-theory assumptions permitted by the user.
  The bounded threshold application is proved with threshold 441/4.
  The Gaussian confining application is in GaussianPlanar.lean.
  Neither library branch assumes the manuscript's first two levels.

AxialRealization.lean
  StandardAxialLibrary supplies general full-line realization, Sturm
  simplicity, nonzero spectral correspondence for first-order products, and
  the free constant-potential spectrum. The explicit sech ground state,
  normalization, finite energy, constant partner and gap c^2 are proved.
  The spectrum and multiplicity metadata are identified with OperatorSpectrum
  definitions, and both operators have explicit selfadjoint graph properties.

TensorRealization.lean
  StandardTensorLibrary supplies the universal selfadjoint tensor-sum theorem.
  Inputs include the actual smooth coefficient conditions for both factors.
  Output includes compactly supported test functions in the operator domain
  with the literal three-dimensional differential action, a weak equation,
  the actual spectrum sum, and the general unique-sum dimension formula.
  Its separated-function law is quantified over arbitrary energies, radial
  and axial eigenstates, scalar multiples and literal product representatives.
  Both manuscript Cartesian orbitals are identified with those representatives
  using their proved cylindrical pullback formulas. This identification is
  a proof, not a premise that the selected orbitals are eigenfunctions.
  For c^2 > 20, exact simple levels 0 and 20 and a strict upper gap follow.

FermionRealization.lean
  The state space is the actual kernel of swap+identity in Lebesgue L2 on
  two copies of complex-plane times real-line configuration space.
  StandardFermionLibrary is the general exterior-square filling theorem for
  arbitrary isolated simple first two levels and arbitrary orthonormal
  eigenfunctions. It includes a selfadjoint operator on this antisymmetric
  space, its literal differential sum action on smooth compact tests, and
  its weak differential equation. The general filling theorem is external
  permitted operator theory, not a theorem proved from mathlib here.
  The bounded_ground_state application uses the actual IVT-selected parameter,
  actual Cartesian eigenfunctions, actual orthonormality, and the literal
  determinant from ConcreteSlater. Its ground energy is 20 and its eigenspace
  has complex dimension one and is isolated. The general exterior filling
  theorem returns this isolation from the assumed one-body gap.
  Normalization, positive density, and vanishing
  physical current are all part of the conclusion.
  The bounded_example wrapper fixes c=5 and adds actual smoothness, global
  bounds, and nonzero planar curl at the same parameter t.

Scope
  These files verify the bounded +A noninteracting realization relative to
  the explicitly stated general operator-theory library propositions.
  They do not assert that mathlib itself already provides these libraries.
  The final theorem here does not assemble complex-conjugation covariance
  for the -A partner, the Gaussian three-dimensional exterior-power theorem,
  or the interacting monopole Hamiltonian. Those assemblies are provided by
  NoninteractingTheorem and MonopoleCounterexample. See the project-wide
  COVERAGE.txt and ASSUMPTIONS.txt for their proof chains and trust boundary.
