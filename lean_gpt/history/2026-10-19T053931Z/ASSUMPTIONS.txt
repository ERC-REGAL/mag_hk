Background assumptions and trust boundary

The user permits well-known operator theory, elliptic regularity and unique
continuation. General results in those areas appear as explicit Lean theorem
parameters. They are not silently installed instances or new Lean axioms.
The conclusions are relative to these stated background facts. The axiom
audit does not discharge theorem parameters.

Read the named definitions for their exact quantifiers and hypotheses. The
state spaces, forms, differential expressions, spectra and marginal integrals
are literal mathematical objects. Spectrum means failure of a bounded two-sided
resolvent. Selfadjointness means equality with the Hilbert adjoint graph.
